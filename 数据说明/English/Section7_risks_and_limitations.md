## 7.2 Other issues seen in the actual results

1. **The effective sample size is far below 230,000.** For samples 1 minute apart, the long-30-minute label is the same 85% of the time, and it drops to the random level only once the gap reaches the holding period (Figure 3b). Roughly counting one independent sample per 60 minutes, the training set is equivalent to only about 4,000 independent observations. Overfitting is easy, and validation metrics will fluctuate more than the sample count suggests. Response: strong regularization and early stopping; report the mean and range over several random seeds.
2. **Only one year of data and only one instrument.** The test set covers only about 2 months (2026-08-06 to 09-29), with no roll and no turbulent market like the one from late January to early February 2026. Test results represent only this market period.
3. **Label precision is limited by the 1-minute granularity.** When both lines are touched in the same minute, the label is stop-loss (0.02%–0.07%). The entry price uses the best bid and best ask at the open of minute t+1 (a quote at one instant), not the average spread over that minute, so for some samples the entry spread is smaller or larger than the typical spread of that minute, and a live order may not get this price. The label audit verifies that the logic was executed correctly; it did not use tick data to check the order of events within a minute.
4. **Degraded-quality days are kept, but problems cannot be fully ruled out.** The 8 dates flagged by Databento have 3,419 rows in total (all in the training set, about 1.4%); they are kept and marked with `flag_degraded_day`. On the charts the market looks normal, with no missing minutes, and volume matches the official bars; but the official bars and MBP-1 come from the same Databento capture, so if messages were missed, both would miss them, and this check is not independent evidence. Response: in M4, retrain once on a training set without these days; if the difference is within the range of random-seed variation, they do not affect the conclusions.
