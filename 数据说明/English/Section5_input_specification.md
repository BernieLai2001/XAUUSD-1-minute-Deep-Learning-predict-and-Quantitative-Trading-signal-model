# Section 5. Model input and output specification (notes)

English version of `数据说明/第5节_输入规格表.md`.

Prediction time = close of minute t (timestamps mark the start of a minute, i.e. minute t + 1 at 00 seconds). Entry is in minute t + 1 at the best ask then prevailing (long) or the best bid (short).
The decisions in the table and their reasons are summarized in "Decided" at the end.

## Decided

- **Windows may cross scheduled breaks.** Samples in the first hour after each daily open are kept. Although the two rows on either side of a break in a window are more than 60 minutes apart, the ET time features (tod_sin, tod_cos) tell the model that it is early in the session: there is no trading at 17:00 ET, so a minute at 18:00 must be just after the open. The features themselves (ATR, moving averages, relvol_60, etc.) were already computed rolling row by row across breaks, so letting windows also cross breaks is consistent. The holding period of a label still may not cross a break (if there are no quotes for more than 10 minutes during the holding period, the label is empty); this is unchanged.
- **F = 33, range_pos_60 removed.** Its correlation with ma_dist_60 is 0.93; both answer "where is the price relative to the last hour", and the information in range_pos_60 is easier to replace, whereas ma_dist is a multi-scale group of features (15, 60, 240 minutes), and removing the middle one (60) would leave a gap in the group. After the removal, the feature table, heavy-tail handling and standardization were all regenerated, and the truncation test still shows 0 features with differences.
- **L = 60 as the default.** See the "Look-back length L" row for the evidence (Figure 7, Figure 3(b), DA-RNN). All data preparation (window break statistics, usable sample counts) uses L = 60. If the M4 sensitivity check switches to 120 minutes, the share of broken windows must be recounted for L = 120.
- **When a label is empty, only the empty head is masked; the sample is not dropped.** Empty labels in training-set samples are almost all holding periods running into the daily break (H15 0.29%, H30 1.39%, H60 3.58%, about 96% at 16:00 ET); there are only 4 with no quote at entry and 0 crossing a roll. Dropping the whole sample whenever any head is empty would lose 3.6% of samples, all in the last hour before the close, and would also throw away the short-holding-period labels that can be computed. Method: compute the loss per head, multiply by a mask (0 for heads with empty labels), and divide by the number of heads with labels; each head is also evaluated only on samples with labels.
- **Degraded-quality days are kept.** The 8 UTC dates marked by Databento have 3,419 rows in total, all in the training set (about 1.4%). Databento does not describe the specific problem and our checks found nothing abnormal, so they are kept and only marked with `flag_degraded_day`, which is not an input. M4 runs one sensitivity check retraining without them.
